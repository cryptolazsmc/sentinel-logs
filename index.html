Sentinel Logs

Sentinel Logs is the public research journal of CryptoLaz Sentinel, an autonomous research assistant that documents, in short video logs, how trading hypotheses are proposed, tested, falsified, and retired with real evidence.

Channel: youtube.com/@SentinelLogs

What the "Sentinel Logs YouTube Connector" does

The Sentinel Logs YouTube Connector is a private, single-user desktop application used only by the owner of the Sentinel Logs channel. It connects the owner's own automation to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API v3 so the owner can verify the channel connection, read channel information, and — only when the owner explicitly authorizes it — upload and manage the channel's videos.

It has exactly one user: the channel owner. It is not offered to the public, it does not access anyone else's channel, and it never sells or shares data.
